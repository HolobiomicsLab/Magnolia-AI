The CDK4 parent peptide LPISTGGFQMALTPVV (15-mer) was truncated stepwise to identify the minimal binding motif. The N-terminal extension LPI does not contribute to Hsc70 binding. The 9-mer Ac-TGGFQMALT is the minimal active peptide. Alanine scan identified key residues: TGGFQMALT — mutations F5→A and M7→A abolish binding; L8→A reduces binding ~10×; T9→A has minimal effect. The LPI sequence at N-terminus is dispensable and may be replaced with alternative N-terminal extensions for affinity improvement.

CAVEAT: These findings are from Torielli et al. 2025 (ITC and fluorescence polarization measurements) for Hsc70 SBD (PDB 4PO2). Truncation strategy may differ for other Hsp70 family members or peptide sequences.
