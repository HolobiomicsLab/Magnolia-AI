The CDK4 parental 15-mer LPISTGGFQMALTPVV was truncated stepwise: first to 11-mer (STGGFQMALTP), then to 9-mer (TGGFQMALT). The 9-mer TGGFQMALT retained high affinity (Kd ~20 nM) and was the minimum active sequence. Alanine scanning showed that residues Phe4, Met6, and Leu8 are critical for binding (ΔΔG > 2 kcal/mol), while N-terminal residues LPIS are dispensable. The truncation improved affinity 50-fold over the original 15-mer.

CAVEAT: These results come from surface plasmon resonance (SPR) measurements on Hsc70 SBD. The truncation strategy may not transfer to other Hsp70 homologs or to peptides with different binding motifs.
